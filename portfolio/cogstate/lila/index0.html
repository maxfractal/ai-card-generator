Project Overview

The Challenge

The creative brief for Lila was beautifully simple: take one of Cogstate’s most successful games used in clinical trials and make a consumer version of it for smartphones.

User Research

However, execution of the idea was actually more creatively challenging than originally realized for everyone involved— especially when it came to the value proposition of taking our “International Shopping List Test” game, the gold standard for cognitive assessment in clinical trials, and commercializing it for a broad non-academic audience.

Personas

Once past the first design sprint with our in-house scientists, I interviewed five subjects to provide real-world input on the ideas taking form.

Due to sensitivity around privacy concerns, I created proto-personas to keep the user at the center of the process without exposing any individual’s identity.

Wireframes

Wireframes were created to represent the tasks for each of the primary scenarios. These low-fidelity screens were also used to create an early, interactive prototype for stakeholder reviews.

Wireflow: Device Check

Working with key stakeholders on the Cogstate science team, I created a series of low-fidelity user flows to prototype how people move from device check into the game itself.

Wireframes: Iterations

Once stakeholders could interact with the prototype, feedback quickly surfaced opportunities to refine both the UX and UI. I iterated on several details in the interface while also beginning to layer in branding and visual language.

Wireframes: High-Fidelity

From there, I evolved the interactive prototype into a fully branded experience, integrating visual design, color, and typography to bring Lila’s identity to life.
