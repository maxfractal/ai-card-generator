Case Study — Product Design

Paris 2024 Olympics App

Designing a unified, metadata-driven experience for Comcast Xfinity, Verizon Fios, Dish Network, and Sling — bringing live, replay, and highlights together with consistent UI and real-time updates.

Design Systems Metadata UX Live/VOD UX Partner Enablement Accessibility

Objective

Deliver a seamless Olympics streaming experience across partner apps so fans can discover, watch, and return to live and on-demand moments — without friction. Build a component-driven system that adapts to real-time schedules, athlete stories, and platform constraints.

  • Platforms: Comcast Xfinity, Verizon Fios, Dish Network, Sling TV
  • Role: Senior Product UX Manager — strategy, IA, design system, prototyping, partner reviews
  • Focus: Findability, live/replay flow, artwork automation, cross-platform consistency

Problem

Different partners, different systems — one Olympics. The UI needed to be consistent, responsive to real-time events, and easy for partners to ship quickly.

  • Varied navigation patterns and tech stacks across partners
  • Thousands of events updating daily; manual curation was not feasible
  • Brand and accessibility standards had to be enforced at scale

Approach

  • Metadata-Driven UX: Connected Gracenote + Connector APIs to fill hero banners, trays, and athlete modules automatically.
  • Adaptive Design System: Built Olympic-themed components (hero, carousels, pictograms, Up Next) that reflowed across STBs, CTVs, and mobile apps.
  • Cross-Partner Alignment: Published PX guidelines and reviewed builds with Comcast, Verizon, Dish/Sling to maintain consistency.
  • Personalization & Discovery: “Up Next” module surfaced ongoing events by locale, sport, and schedule context.
  • QA & Compliance: Automated artwork checks and aPX audits to reduce validation time and ensure accessibility.
