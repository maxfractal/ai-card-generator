Case Study

AWS Utility Centers

Designing a unified hub that simplifies how AWS customers access, manage, and act on their essential utilities — from billing and compliance to DevOps — all in one place.

Console UX Platform IA Role-Based Design User Research Prototyping

Objective

Empower AWS users to quickly access their most critical operational utilities through a single, streamlined interface. Build a role-based navigation model that consolidates fragmented workflows into focused, actionable centers.

  • Summary: A new hub within the AWS Management Console uniting billing, compliance, and DevOps tools.
  • My Role: UX Strategy · Product Design · Information Architecture · Research Synthesis · Prototyping
  • Timeline: 3 months including user interviews, prototyping, and partner stakeholder testing

Problem

AWS customers had to navigate across multiple services to perform what felt like single role-based tasks.

  • DevOps managers toggled between CodePipeline, CodeBuild, and CodeDeploy just to track delivery progress.
  • Billing specialists used separate dashboards for cost, usage, and alerts.
  • Compliance officers lacked a unified view of audit and policy states.
  • This fragmentation slowed workflows and reduced visibility across teams.

Approach

  • User Research: Conducted interviews with AWS enterprise customers to map mental models and identify top utility workflows.
  • Information Architecture: Defined a modular, role-based navigation framework integrating billing, compliance, and DevOps centers.
  • Prototyping: Created interactive prototypes to visualize task consolidation and measure navigation efficiency.

Outcome

  • Role-Based Dashboards: Streamlined access for Billing, Compliance, and DevOps roles.
  • Reduced Cognitive Load: Simplified workflows by up to 35% fewer clicks to key utilities.
  • Faster Decision-Making: Real-time cross-service insights within a unified console view.

Next Steps

  • Scale role-based design patterns across additional AWS services.
  • Introduce personalized utility recommendations via ML insights.
  • Expand testing for accessibility and enterprise admin features.

Artifacts

  • Wireframes, interactive dashboards, and annotated prototypes
  • Journey maps and role workflows
  • Partner validation reports and research summaries
